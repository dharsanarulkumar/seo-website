Bandhini Sarees – ₹680

Bandhini sarees are more than garments — they are living expressions of India’s rich textile heritage. Originating from Rajasthan and Gujarat, a traditional Bandhini saree is crafted using the centuries-old tie-and-dye technique known as Bandhej. Artisans hand-tie thousands of tiny knots before dyeing the fabric, creating intricate dotted motifs unique to every saree. At Colours Overload, our Bandhini collection blends authentic craftsmanship with modern versatility. Ideal for office wear, festive occasions, and cultural celebrations, these handcrafted sarees balance tradition and contemporary elegance. Lightweight and breathable, they offer all-day comfort while maintaining a graceful drape. Choosing a Bandhej saree means embracing heritage, artisan skill, and timeless Indian craftsmanship.
